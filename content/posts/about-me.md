**I am a deaf and blind computer programmer and use my refreshable Braille display to read and access all information online. It is like a keyboard and allows me to be successful in writing programs.**





**I am motivated to help people with disabilities to improve their lives. I persevere in finding a solution and develop software applications because I have a lot of knowledge about disabilities and understand how disabled persons struggle in their lives.   **





**One of my current favorite projects is one using Python Flask and youtube-transcript-api to develop a website application that extracts transcripts from YouTube. This way I can read the transcript of a video rather than attempt to access closed captions using my Braille display. **





**I am very interested in developing a mobile application can be replaced with a software remote for driverless cars  that would be user-friendly and accessible to everyone regardless of disability. **





**I enjoy looking ahead to when I and others will create new applications making more of life’s day to day activities easier by being accessible for everyone! Thank you for reading my blog post.**
