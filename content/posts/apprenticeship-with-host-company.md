I am apprenticing with my host company, Worthwhile through the Develop Carolina program since January 2022.





I have had a great experience with Worthwhile. My assigned mentor is very knowledgeable and helpful that I received a lot of information. She told me about React and Vue.js. Vue.js is recommended for my umbrella project. I plan to use it to build my web app with Python and Flask.  





I chatted with her via Google Chat three times a week. I specifically asked questions that I’ve been having pertaining to the SC Codes curriculum. 





Additionally, I have looked through a project in GitLab and tried to get familiar with it.





I hope gaining new experience and contributing to a real project is beneficial. I’m excited to see that I’ll have a lot of knowledgeable with Vue.js as well as from the SC Codes curriculum.
