I enjoyed pencil drawing as a child and on through college age. I had enough vision at that time. 





I was determined to create an accurate image as an artist, because I enjoyed perfecting the details in my work. Focusing on 3D and grayscale were my favorite areas of pencil drawing. Granted, a large illustration would take many hours to finish but I was persistent. If I made a mistake while drawing, I would find a creative way to hide it by working it into the final piece making it more beautiful than if it had been perfectly drawn.





Another enjoyable aspect of drawing was thinking critically studying and analyzing the principles of art, such as proportion, movement or rhythm.   





Comparing creating art to software engineering, in each, I start from scratch and turn my thought processes to the idea for the project. Both have standards that should be included and yet have a place where creativity allows for new details to be injected into the application or final piece of art.  Unlike drawing, when I find a bug in my application, I have to do some research until I find a way to completely get rid of the error in the coding. <br />
