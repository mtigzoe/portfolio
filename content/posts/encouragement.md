Dear Future Self,





I am thinking about making a series of YouTube videos on a regular basis outlining my life as a DeafBlind person. Do you think that 





people would be interested? Yes, indeed! 





I definitely agree with you! I am so excited about that. It would be great for people to know the fabulous quality of life I live as a DeafBlind person. I’ll show them the ways God has provided for me to successfully manage all the details of daily living. They’ll be fascinated by the technology I use to access information and communication on the internet.





I plan to share my accomplishments regarding developing my software applications when I work as a software developer. I feel confident that i will work hard and help improve people’s lives. Caring about others is important to me.





I will be helpful to others one day via this series. Thank you for reading this look back at my future plans to enhance the lives of others.





Sincerely,





Author
