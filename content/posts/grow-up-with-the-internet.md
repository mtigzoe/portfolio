I was introduced to Google for the first time when I was in the 3rd grade. My teacher taught me how to use it to look at images for an assignment. However, I didn’t use the internet much until I started middle school. Since then much of my schooling has been completed utilizing it.





Reading books online is a favorite hobby of mine. Using MSN Dictionary on the internet has helped me a lot as I came across new words while reading. I also frequently check my emails, and research on the internet. My keyboarding skills continued improving. 





I can’t imagine how I would live without the internet! God has blessed me by allowing me to live at a time and in a place where I have  the technology for the visually impaired to access massive amounts of information.
