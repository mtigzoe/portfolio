I am a deaf and blind computer programmer and my learning process is different from a sighted person. Therefore, I must take more time to learn new concepts.





When reading codes, I usually read one line at a time using my Braille display. Because i cannot quickly look back at them,  I have to memorize them while reading through files. If an error message occurs, I must try to remember where codes are located and then fix them.





I use Google, GitHub, Slackoverflow, and blogs to help me solve problems. Using my screen reader hotkeys allows me to navigate websites easier. 





When I research online and find helpful resources, I copy and paste the links to a text file  so that I can use them later. Additionally, copying code samples from tutorials helps me understand them better when I’m learning a new computer language.





If I am stuck and am not able to find a solution, I will get away from my computer for a moment. I attempt to find a way to solve it. I am usually able to figure it out. 





My mentors provide brief explanations when I am presented with new materials.





I am motivated to succeed  and have persevered to reach my computer programming career goals.
