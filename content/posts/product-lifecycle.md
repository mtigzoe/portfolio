After two-hour lecture, I would like to share what I learned about the topics in Product Lifecycle. Five topics include Product Market Fit, stages of a company, product lifecycle stages, inter-team coordination and intra-team coordination.       





Product Market Fit





A product satisfies a strong market demand.





Stages of a Company





The company has progressive goals as its goals are to find market fit.





<ol class="wp-block-list"><li>Start – A startup company searches for Product Market Fit.</li><li>Scale – a growth stage company finds the Product Market Fit and scales it.</li><li>Sustain – An enterprise company seeks to sustain the success of the product market fit.</li><li>Shifts – Enterprise company-corporate innovation looks for a new one.</li></ol>



Product Lifecycle Stages





New Product is to create the first version of the product.





Growth is to scale and sustain a product.





Product Expansion is to keep the product stable and flexible.





Harvesting is to ensure ease of use and keep risk low.





Inter-team Coordination





Product teams connect to stakeholders. Teams have agreements to work together.





Intra-team Coordination





Intra-team coordination is a product team that works with a product manager, a product designer, a lead engineer, and data analyst.   





When I learn more about the product lifecycle, I will be able to understand the goals of my company. It allows me to work effectively with my team. Product Lifecycle is definitely the important concept that helps me succeed with my company.
