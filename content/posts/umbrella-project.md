I have been thinking about developing the accessible ti-89 calculator web app for visually impaired and blind students who take calculus classes. I am very motivated to help them succeed. 





I found that Orbit Research has the physical ti-84 graphing calculator, but it does not include the software. 





I chose to use Flask and Vue to develop the web app and to copy features from a regular ti-89 calculator. Features on the web app should be accessible and readable. Viewing a graphing calculator will need to have a high contrast.





The web app for the ti-89 calculator should be user-friendly, accessible and inexpensive, so that schools can purchase it for their visually impaired and blind students. I am excited to see that students will be successful as they use the app to enhance their education.





I am looking forward to developing it!
