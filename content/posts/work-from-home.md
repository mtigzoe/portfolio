I work from 8am to 5pm every day, Monday to Friday. My computer is powered off every night, so that it is ready for the next day.  





When working remotely, I use my refreshable Braille display and JAWS to access all the information on my computer. When I research and look for tutorials to help me solve a problem on my projects, I usually record the links for my resources, so that I can review them later. Microsoft Visual Studio is my favorite text editor and typing commands in PowerShell works well for me.





During my lunch break, I go away from my computer and prepare my mail. I enjoy taking a walk for exercise and rest before I return to my computer. If I get tired and need to take a short break, I get a healthy snack, drink some water and stretch. 





When I come upon a difficult part of my project, I think and research on the internet until I get good ideas to try to solve the problem. Also, checking Slack and emails helps me refresh my mind while taking a break from my project.





It’s important for me to maintain my mental health as I continue to focus on my work. I enjoy working as a developer from home.
